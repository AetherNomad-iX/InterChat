# Small-business web retainers

## Question

Is a demo-first, ~$300/month web-management retainer a viable offer for local operators (tree services, lawn, property)?

## Sources

| Source | Kind | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Owner working number | primary | $300/mo per site, stated across 2026 chats; not a signed rate card |
| [state/projects/wise-guides.md](../../state/projects/wise-guides.md) | derived | Project file |
| Public repos `willis-property-services`, `noahs-ark-lawn` | artifact | Demos/sites in the same motion; do not invent performance claims |

## Claims

1. **Demo-first beats a proposal deck** for this owner: he already sells in person and can show a phone. Confidence: **medium-high**.
2. **$300/mo is in the "small enough to say yes, large enough to matter" band** for a single local operator site, assuming the owner does not hide 20 hours of custom app work inside it. Confidence: **medium**.
3. **The product is maintenance + small edits, not a new product company every month.** Confidence: **high** as a *should*, unproven as delivery.
4. **Without an inventory of live demos in this hub, we cannot tune the offer.** Confidence: **high**.

## What this implies

- Next research step is not more pricing theory. It is a list of real URLs.
- Claude should write the one-pager only after the inventory exists, or write it as clearly labeled draft copy.
- Do not publish fake case studies.

## Open

What is in the $300 (hosting, edits/month, uptime, content)? That belongs in the offer, then in a decision log when the owner locks it.
